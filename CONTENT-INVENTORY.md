## FAQ (from /faq) - real answers, pulled by expanding every accordion item

- **Cost:** CSA-Xenia is a tuition-free public school.

## Mission, vision, philosophy (from /details and /info)

**Mission:** "The Community STE(A)M Academy - Xenia's mission is to educate the whole student through innovative, student-centered, relevant learning experiences to meet communities' current and future needs by embracing the rigorous, interdisciplinary, problem-solving STEM pathway while embedding the creativity of the Arts."

**What is STEAM at CSA (paraphrased from /info):** STEAM isn't a single activity that touches science, tech, engineering, arts, or math individually; it's project-based learning that merges all five so students engage with real community concerns instead of memorizing for a test. ## Projects (from /projects)

Only one project post exists on the live site so far: **"Lunar Recovery Mission - A Workforce Direct Project"** by Dr. Jeremy Ervin, May 29. This is a genuinely exceptional real accomplishment worth featuring prominently, not buried:

CSA-Xenia students, in collaboration with the **Wright Brothers Institute (WBI)** and the **Air Force Research Laboratory (AFRL)**, worked on a real initiative connected to **NASA's Artemis program**: "Lunar Recovery Mission: High Angle Recovery of Personnel and Equipment," designing rescue solutions for astronauts and equipment on the lunar surface near Shackleton Crater at the lunar south pole.

- All CSA-Xenia students participated in the initial Design Thinking phase; interested students then joined one of **three Design Teams**.
- Work included market research on commercial lunar surface vehicles, mechanical/environmental analysis of winch performance, and exploring robotic arms/unmanned systems for recovery.
- Students built simulations in **Unreal Engine**, including a live demonstration of a zip-line recovery concept, plus CAD/Blender component models and detailed reports.
- Each of the three Design Teams presented their solution; there's a combined slide deck deliverable referenced on the original page.
- The project culminated in students presenting to WBI and **MSgt Ben Johnis**, who is actively involved in the real Artemis mission.
- A "sprint coach" reflection is included on organizing teams, fostering creative risk-taking, mentorship, and growth mindset under a tight timeline.
