Admissions

Enrollment is open for grades 4-9.

Every family's journey starts with an application. CSA-Xenia admits students on a rolling basis if seats are available, and all applications are reviewed before acceptance.

Requirements

Who can enroll

  • Student is a current resident of Ohio, or will be after relocation
  • Student is not currently under suspension or expulsion from another public or nonpublic school
  • Student is not currently subject to disciplinary exclusion for conduct involving weapons, violence, or illegal drugs
  • Student is in good standing at their current school, meaning eligible for promotion based on participation and conduct, not academic performance

Students with patterns of chronic absenteeism, truancy, or refusal to engage in learning activities may not be admitted, or may be required to complete a support-planning process before or upon enrollment.
