Informational Meeting

An enrollment info session held at the Greene County Public Library - Xenia Branch while the Church Street building was being prepared for students.

What Love Can Do

An original theater production written, produced, and performed entirely by CSA students in the theater elective class.

2026-27: What's Next for CSA?

Bunny Bolt: Circuit Approved Fun Run

Laughter Lab Student Workshop & LIVE

Student-Led Parent-Teacher Conferences
