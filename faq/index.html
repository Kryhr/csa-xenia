FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Answers to the questions families ask most often about enrollment, transportation, grading, and daily life at CSA.

Does it cost anything to attend?

No. CSA-Xenia is a tuition-free public school.

Is CSA-Xenia part of the Xenia school district?

No. CSA-Xenia is an independent public STEAM-Designated school approved by the Ohio Department of Education, not part of the Xenia Community City School District.

Who can attend?

Any Ohio resident (or family relocating to Ohio) who is not currently suspended or expelled, not subject to disciplinary exclusion for weapons, violence, or drugs, and in good standing, defined by participation and conduct rather than grades.

What about transportation?

If a student lives within a 30-minute bus-ride radius of their home-district school, that district must provide bussing or financial compensation to and from CSA-Xenia, similar to the Greene County Career Center's arrangement.

Does CSA offer sports and clubs?

CSA can't replicate sports already offered by a student's home district, but offers Esports, Competitive Jump Roping, Design Challenges, Baking Cookoffs, and Drone Racing, with Fine Arts, 4-H, and Amateur Radio clubs planned based on student interest.

What are the school hours and calendar?

The school day runs 7:45am-2:45pm. See the full 2026-27 Calendar and Explanations PDF.

How does the grade rollout work?

Grades 6-9 began in the first year (2023-24); grades 3-4 were added in 2025-26; the inaugural graduating class is Year 4 (2026-27); K-3 is anticipated after the Church Street transition completes.

Can I volunteer?

Yes. CSA runs a volunteer system through Community Experts, overseen by a Community Enterprise office.

What does project-based learning actually look like day-to-day?

Teachers pre-assess students, then group them for cross-discipline projects, with explicit instruction mixed in when it's the most effective strategy.

How does grading work?

Grading is ongoing, multi-method assessment throughout unit-based projects, discussions, interviews, writing, and performances, rather than single quizzes or tests.

Does CSA take state tests?

Yes. CSA-Xenia is a public school and administers the required Ohio state tests, though CSA values them as one measure among several, alongside creativity, problem-solving, and collaboration.

What about college prep?

CSA uses Ohio's College Credit Plus program; different graduation pathways offer different numbers of CCP options, alongside CTE coursework.

How can I give input to the Board?

Through the Partners page or the Contact page.

What's school lunch like?

CSA participates in the National School Lunch Program. A sample week: chicken nuggets and soft pretzel, tacos, egg rolls with chicken and rice, turkey wrap, meatball sandwich, with every meal including a vegetable, fruit, and milk.

What's the screen time policy?

Technology is used with purpose: heavier at the start of the year for platform onboarding, then balanced with hands-on and group work as the year progresses.

Is before/after care available?

It's evaluated annually based on demonstrated family demand and, when offered, is available for an additional fee.

What's on the 2026-27 school supply list?

Supply lists are posted by grade band: 4th-6th grade, 7th-8th grade, and high school.

What's the policy on mobile phones and internet use?

Governed by Policy 4440 (Mobile Electronic Devices) and Policy 4430 (Internet/Technology Acceptable Use) in the Policy Manual of the Governing Authority.

Is there a dress code?

Dress and grooming is governed by Policy 4420, in the Policy Manual of the Governing Authority.

What's the maximum enrollment per grade?

Anticipated maximum enrollment by neighborhood and grade band is on the About page.

What does a typical day look like?

Every day mixes cross-discipline, project-based work with more traditional instruction where that's the most effective way to teach a given skill. Teachers pre-assess students, then group them for projects that connect multiple subjects at once.

Where can I find the Student Wellness and Success Funds Plan?

The full Disadvantaged Pupil Impact Aid / Student Wellness and Success Funds Plan is available as a PDF.

Still have a question?

Reach out directly and CSA will get back to you.
