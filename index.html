Real student work

Students designed a lunar rescue system with the Wright Brothers Institute and the Air Force Research Lab

CSA-Xenia students worked on "Lunar Recovery Mission: High Angle Recovery of Personnel and Equipment," a real initiative connected to NASA's Artemis program, designing rescue solutions for astronauts near the lunar south pole.

  • Three student Design Teams researched vehicles, winch performance, and robotic recovery systems
  • Students built Unreal Engine simulations, including a live zip-line recovery demonstration
  • The project culminated in a presentation to WBI and an active-duty Artemis mission service member
