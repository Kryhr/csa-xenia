Military Family Resources

A Purple Star School.

CSA-Xenia holds the Ohio Department of Education and Workforce's Purple Star School designation (Class of 2025), recognizing schools that meet the Purple Star Advisory Board's standards for supporting military-connected students and families.

Your point of contact

Megan Gebhart-Powell serves as CSA's Military Family Liaison, in addition to her role as Associate Director.

mgebhartpowell@communitysteam.com

Resources for military-connected families

Wright-Patterson Military & Family Readiness Center

937-257-3592
Walk-in hours: Mon-Fri, 7:30am-4:30pm.
Closed weekends, federal holidays, and the first Thursday of each month for staff training.

MIC3

The Military Interstate Children's Compact Commission, supporting military-connected students through school transitions between states.

Blue Star Families - Dayton Chapter

A local chapter of the national nonprofit connecting and supporting military families.

Greene County Parks & Rec, ESC, and Library

Local educational classes and community resources available to CSA families through Greene County: Parks & Rec, ESC, Library.
