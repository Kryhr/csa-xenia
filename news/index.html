Current announcement

New First Day of School & Orientation Date

We are excited to welcome our students and families to the 2026-2027 school year at our originally planned location, 135 East Church St. (the former YMCA building). As we continue preparing the facility, construction delays require us to adjust the beginning of our school year.

First Day of School: Monday, September 14, 2026.
Orientation: Thursday, September 10, 2026, 5-8pm. Our previously scheduled two Orientation/Open House days are combined into this one evening.

We do not anticipate extending the school year past Friday, May 28. The Board will consider adjusting "Online Learning @ Home" days to address the affected instructional time, not the typical holidays and breaks. Thank you for your patience and flexibility as we prepare to welcome everyone home to Church Street.

Written by Kim Haines, Aug. 21, 2026
