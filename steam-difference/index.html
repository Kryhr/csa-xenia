Why CSA?

What STEAM actually means at CSA.

STEAM isn't a single activity that touches science, tech, engineering, arts, or math individually. At CSA, it's project-based learning that merges all five so students engage with real community concerns instead of memorizing for a test, preparing them for jobs that don't exist yet: the Institute for the Future estimates that roughly 85% of the jobs students will hold in 2030 haven't been invented.

Learning is organized by neighborhood, not just grade level

Instead of a single grade-level classroom, CSA groups students into named "neighborhoods," each with its own coaches and a low student-to-teacher ratio built for individualized attention. It's also why staff titles like "Life Skills Neighborhood Coach" exist on the staff page.

Real-world credentials, not just a diploma

Older students can use Ohio's College Credit Plus program to earn college credit alongside their diploma, and different graduation pathways at CSA open up different numbers of CCP options, alongside CTE (Career-Technical Education) coursework and workforce credentials.
