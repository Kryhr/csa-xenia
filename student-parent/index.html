Student & Parent Info

Everything families need to know.

Attendance, technology, clubs, and calendars, all in one place.

Attendance

If your student will be out for the day, email attendance@communitysteam.com or call (937) 800-2720 and leave a message on the attendance line.

School schedule

The school day runs 7:45am-2:45pm. Doors open 30 minutes before, at 7:15am. Students are expected off school premises 30 minutes after, by 3:15pm. Early drop-off and late pick-up may carry a fee; contact office@communitysteam.com.

Digital Academy

CSA recently switched from a mix of platforms (Final Forms, OTUS, MySchoolBucks, Class Dojo) to a single web-based learning management system, Digital Academy, covering lunch ordering and parent/teacher communication. If you haven't received setup instructions, email info@communitysteam.com.

Technology & internet use

A signed Internet & Acceptable Use Policy and a $125 Technology Use fee are required before a student receives a school laptop. To maximize instructional time, devices generally stay at school during the week and go home only for scheduled online-learning days.

After-school clubs & PIVOT

CSA offers after-school clubs including Robotics, Role Playing Games, Creative Arts, and Creative Crafters, based on student interest. Parents and Teachers in Volunteering and Organizing Together (PIVOT), started November 2025, is CSA's PTO/PTA-style family organization; email info@communitysteam.com to get involved.

Extended care

Before- and after-school care is evaluated for demand each year and offered for an additional fee when there's sufficient interest. Email info@communitysteam.com for current availability.
