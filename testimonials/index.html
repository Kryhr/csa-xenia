Testimonials

What families, partners, and board members say about CSA.

Real voices from the CSA community

 

"I am so very grateful that my daughter is looking forward to school again. I can't tell you what a blessing it is."

New 2024-25 Parent

Shared after discussing a difficult year for their family

 

"As the year at CSA Xenia has progressed we have witnessed a major transformation in our son. He has transitioned from a reluctant student to an excited academic participant as he takes ownership of his education and continues to learn how to learn. His personal growth has also excelled due to the many opportunities to interact and collaborate with other students while completing projects. We eagerly anticipate the coming years."

Proud CSA Parents

CSA family

"As the Founder of the Wright Brothers Institute, I am privileged to applaud the exceptional educational paradigm at Community Steam Academy-Xenia. Their integration of Arts into STEM, commitment to entirely project-based learning, and a steadfast focus on community needs result in graduates equipped not just with academic prowess but with critical thinking and collaborative skills essential for today's dynamic business environment. This institution is not merely shaping students; it is cultivating responsible, innovative, and socially aware leaders poised to make a meaningful impact."

Dr. Bart Barthelemy

Founder, Wright Brothers Institute

"Asking Who? What? When? Where? and Why? about everyday life can lead to exciting adventures as the answers are pursued. The goal of educators should always be to make learning the best ongoing adventure ever."

Dr. Kezia Curry

CSA Board Member

"Everyone has a sense of wonder that is usually squelched by our formal education systems. CSA-Xenia will facilitate students to pursue their curiosity and passion by engaging them through active, authentic, and addictive educational experiences that will link with culture and community to allow their in-born sense of wonder to thrive."

Dr. Jeremy Ervin

CSA Founder & CAO

 

"As a business owner invested in the city of Xenia, I've always strived to grow deep roots and do my part in making the local community succeed. By serving as a community partner with CSA-Xenia, I feel as though I have found a meaningful and sustaining way of doing just that."

Ryan Beach

Community Partner

 

"Interacting with students both formally and informally and with students in elementary education and higher education, I realize they all are more motivated and curious when instruction and assessment of learning is engaging and authentic. A STE(A)M approach to learning ignites students' natural desire to problem solve, create, collaborate, and successful completion of a task motivates them for the next challenge."

Dr. Ruth Sylvester

Former CSA Vice-Chair

 

"Because I passionately believe that people are stamped in the image of their Creator and given the planet as a giant sandbox in which to play and create, I am committed to design learning experiences that help people live out who they were created to be and what they were created for."

Jason Hubbard

CSA Secretary
